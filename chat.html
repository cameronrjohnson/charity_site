I care about health and helping people during emergencies. Where should I start?

You could begin by comparing organizations that provide medical care or emergency supplies. Direct Relief is one example in the directory. Check its current programs and reports, then compare with independent sources.

What should I look at before donating?

Look at the organization's mission, recent financial reports, program details, and how it measures results. A directory entry is only a starting point, not a guarantee.
